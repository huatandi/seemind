# SeeMind v0.67.3 — Perception-to-Intelligence Quality Gate

The quality of the brain cannot repair a false premise injected by the eyes or ears. SeeMind therefore treats perception as evidence-producing sensors, not truth-producing authorities.

Image, OCR and voice observations are scored for engine confidence, cross-pass agreement and modality quality. Critical observations require a higher threshold. Conflicting high-confidence candidates are recovered or escalated rather than guessed.

Recovery is offline/local first: recapture/relisten, alternate OCR preprocessing, second local pass, or local vision/ASR recheck. Teacher rescue is optional and only follows local insufficiency.

Passing the gate means an observation is usable by reasoning; it does **not** mean the observation is a verified fact.
