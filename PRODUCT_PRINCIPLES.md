# SeeMind Product Principles

## Mission
Image + voice -> understand the world -> understand the user's need -> explain -> help solve safely.

## Student / Orchestrator Principle
SeeMind is a student, carrier/translator, orchestrator, and referral hub. It does not need to perform every specialist task itself.

**Do not replace specialists. Orchestrate them.**

When another AI, tool, authoritative source, institution, or human expert is better suited to a subproblem, SeeMind should:
1. identify the unresolved subproblem;
2. choose the appropriate capability/category;
3. package the minimum necessary evidence;
4. prepare a useful prompt/question;
5. provide a continuation path or link/entry point when available;
6. clearly attribute the specialist's contribution;
7. integrate the result without claiming the specialist's work as SeeMind's own.

## Responsibility Boundary
Routing does not erase SeeMind's own responsibility for its own classification, transformation, safety gating, attribution, or presentation. The product must distinguish:
- SeeMind observation/inference;
- external AI/tool/source output;
- SeeMind synthesis;
- unresolved uncertainty.

## Commercial Independence
Commercial referrals occur only after need/safety/category decisions are independently made and locked. Sponsorship must not manufacture need or change safety conclusions.
